Sentry robot concept

Drive the robot with the joystick (or W A S D and the arrow keys). Take it into the yellow H2S area to collect a gas sample, then drive up to the valve and open or close it. This is a concept for field review, not a finished design.

    Model: F = 0.5 x 1.225 x Cd x area x speed squared, with Cd 1.2 and wind from the side. Moments are about the leeward track edge on flat ground. Static only, so slopes, gust shock loading, turning and valve reaction forces are not included. The orange arrow in the scene is the gust.

    Parts

    Stowed size is about 1150 long x 660 wide x 910 high (mm). All masses, bay sizes and positions are starting targets, not final numbers. Click a row to isolate that part.

    What we need from the field

    1. Real handwheel diameters, stem heights and breakaway torque for the valves seen on site.
    2. Typical valve centerline heights and clearances around wellheads, manifolds and tank batteries.
    3. The hazardous area classification (zone or division) the robot would work in.
    4. Ground conditions: mud, grating, slopes, curbs and stairs.
    5. Which jobs matter most: valves, gauges, bolts or gas sampling.
    6. Radio or tether limits on real pads.
